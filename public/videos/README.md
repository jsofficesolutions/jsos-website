# Video Assets Folder

Drop your `.mp4` video files into this directory.

### Hero Phone Mockup
To display your video in the 3D phone mockup in the hero section, name your file:
- `hero-scroll-preview.mp4`

Vite serves all files in `public/` from the root URL, so `public/videos/hero-scroll-preview.mp4` will be immediately accessible at `/videos/hero-scroll-preview.mp4`.
